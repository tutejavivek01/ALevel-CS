# Video & Revision Resource Map — AQA Computer Science 7517

Curated from the school's own recommended resource list. These are channel/site-level links,
verified to exist. **None of these are deep links to a specific lesson video for a specific
spec reference (e.g. "the 4.4.2 FSM video") — those weren't verified and should not be
fabricated.** When building topic detail pages, either:
  (a) link to the channel/site's own search or topic-index page for the viewer to find the
      exact lesson themselves, or
  (b) have the app search for and verify a specific video at build/runtime (e.g. via a
      YouTube Data API lookup) rather than hardcoding an unverified video ID, or
  (c) leave a clearly-labelled "search on Craig 'n' Dave for 4.x" affordance instead of a
      fake-looking numbered link.
Do not invent specific YouTube video URLs or IDs — they will 404 or point to the wrong video.

## Primary — spec-mapped video teaching
- **Craig 'n' Dave** — https://www.craigndave.org/ — videos and resources mapped directly to
  the AQA 7517 spec by reference number; this is the closest thing to "the" video series for
  this exact course. Their companion revision site is Smart Revise: https://smartrevise.online/
- **Isaac Computer Science** — https://isaaccomputerscience.org/ — AQA/OCR-aligned content
  with auto-marked questions; strong for programming, algorithms, data structures.

## General programming skill-building (not spec-numbered, but good for 4.1–4.3, 4.12)
- **Corey Schafer** — https://www.youtube.com/@coreyms — deep dives into Python, OOP,
  intermediate concepts.
- **Programming with Mosh** — https://www.youtube.com/@programmingwithmosh
- **Tech With Tim** — https://www.youtube.com/@TechWithTim
- **freeCodeCamp** — https://www.youtube.com/@freecodecamp — long-form full courses.
- **NeetCode** — https://www.youtube.com/@NeetCode — strong for 4.2/4.3 (searches, sorts,
  trees, graphs).
- **CS50 (Harvard)** — https://www.youtube.com/@cs50 — general computer science fundamentals.

## Revision notes & topic-filtered past-paper questions
- **Physics & Maths Tutor** — https://pmt.physicsandmathstutor.com/subject/computer-science
  — AQA-specific topic notes and past-paper questions, filterable by spec section.
- **Save My Exams** — https://www.savemyexams.com/a-level/computer-science/aqa/
- **Seneca Learning** — https://senecalearning.com/ — spaced-repetition revision.
